ECG Replay Research - waveform attribution

The research APK contains 600-second excerpts derived from MIT-BIH records 100
and 207 and Fantasia record f1o01, plus the documented revision noise schedule.
The saved desktop references and phone outputs are transformed versions of
these signals. Units are microvolts, resampled to 250 Hz. The precise source
files, processing recipe and hashes are supplied with the experiment scripts
and manifests. The excerpt and noise transformations are part of this study;
they are not original PhysioNet waveform distributions or clinical ground truth.
